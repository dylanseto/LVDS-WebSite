FADE IN:

1. EXT. CITY STREET — NIGHT

Rain slicks the pavement, neon reflections bleeding into puddles. A figure in a long coat, ARTHUR DRAKE (40s, weathered), moves through the crowd like a man late for his own funeral.

He checks his watch. It has stopped.

ARTHUR
(to himself)

Of course it has.

He ducks into a narrow alley. A MATCH CUT on the flare of a match lighting his cigarette.

CUT TO:

2. INT. 24-HOUR DINER — NIGHT

Fluorescent light. The hum of a refrigerator. A WAITRESS (50s) pours coffee for a sleeping trucker. Arthur slides into a booth.

WAITRESS

Coffee?

ARTHUR

Please. Black.

She pours. He doesn't drink. He stares at the window — his own reflection staring back, older than he remembers.

WAITRESS
(lingering)

You waiting for someone, hon?

ARTHUR

I was. I'm not anymore.

CUT TO:

3. INT. APARTMENT — NIGHT

A sparse room. One chair. A desk littered with photographs. Arthur pins a new picture to the wall — a woman, laughing, mid-sentence.

A PHONE rings. He lets it ring four times before answering.

ARTHUR

Drake.

VOICE (V.O.)
(filtered)

She's not who you think she is, Arthur.

ARTHUR

Who is this?

The line goes dead. Arthur slowly sets the phone down. Outside, a car starts and pulls away.

CUT TO:

4. EXT. ROOFTOP — NIGHT

The city spreads below, a circuit board of light. Arthur stands at the edge. Wind tugs at his coat.

Behind him, footsteps. He doesn't turn.

MAYA (O.S.)

You came.

ARTHUR
(without turning)

I always do.

MAYA CHEN (30s, sharp) steps beside him. She holds a manila envelope.

MAYA

This is everything. After tonight, you don't know me.

ARTHUR

I never did.

She hands him the envelope. Their fingers almost touch. Almost.

CUT TO:

5. INT. UNKNOWN — TIME UNKNOWN

White. Everywhere white. Arthur sits in a single chair, the manila envelope on his lap, unopened.

He looks up.

ARTHUR

So this is how it ends.

A long beat.

He opens the envelope.

Inside: a photograph of himself, on this rooftop, at this moment — taken from behind.

SMASH CUT TO BLACK.

THE END
